Papers

Peter Cotton's papers and notes on epidemics. Shorter pieces are under Essays.

Preprints

  • Cotton, P. (2020). Repeat Contacts and the Spread of Disease: An Agent Model with Compartmental Solution. Preprint. arXiv:2005.10311. SIR with transmission attenuated by the probability that a contact is new, derived from agents moving in the plane. How fast acquaintances go stale changes final sizes by an order of magnitude.
  • Cotton, P. (2020). Addressing the Herd Immunity Paradox Using Symmetry, Convexity Adjustments and Bond Prices. Preprint. arXiv:2006.07341. The growth convexity from the moment generating function of the transmission rate, or Vasicek's bond formula for an Ornstein–Uhlenbeck rate, and the harmonic-mean convexity at the peak. Summarized on mixtures and convexity.

Working papers and notes

  • Cotton, P. (2026). The Immunity Factor from Contact Surveys: Where Epidemics Turn Over. Working paper. PDF · TeX · code and log. The effective reproduction number falls approximately as $R S^\lambda$. A two-year contact panel, under a model of persistent and changing activity, predicts $\lambda = 3.4$-$3.7$ with daily contacts capped at 20 and 4.6-6.8 at larger caps, without fitting to epidemic outcomes; winter US county waves imply about 3.5 at a 0.7% fatality rate. Where contacts were cut, first waves peaked with 1-5% infected.
